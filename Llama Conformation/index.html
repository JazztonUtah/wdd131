    How Trailgrade judges

    Standards lean hard toward athletic pack structure — not show wool or pet types.

    • Sex-specific height bands Males target about 43.5–47″; females 42–45.5″. A shorter female is judged against elite females — not docked for failing male size.
    • Lean weight for the frame Males ideally ~6.5–7.7 lb per inch of height; females ~7.0–8.0. Extra bulk is a disadvantage on the trail — panel targets stay under ~360–400 lb.
    • Legs 2″+ longer than girth depth A Ccara screening disqualifier below 2″. Elite mean sits closer to 3″+ (slightly less for females).
    • Chest under 6″ between forelegs Narrow stance = less wasted energy pacing under pack.
    • Neck ≈ ⅔ of back Balance for counterweight on uneven trail — same for both sexes.
    • Short strong back, level topline Long loins leak hindquarter thrust; high hips shove loads forward.
    • Upright pasterns, sex-appropriate bone, real muscle Females ideal cannon ~5.2–5.9″; males ~5.6–6.25″. Dropped pasterns end packing careers.
